Chitalka 2.02

A major release for calmer reading and a clearer view of your reading rhythm.

• Preview pages and spreads directly from the progress bar with a pointer, drag gesture, or keyboard.
• Optional personal analytics: active time, session history, activity calendar, speed in words and characters, and a completion forecast.
• Analytics syncs through private Google Drive and Yandex Disk app folders; book files are never uploaded.
• A new dedicated settings screen.
• Full-screen status can show the page, percentage, or nothing; page numbers now move with the book.
• Fixed exact EPUB page counting in Safari and illustrations splitting across pages.
