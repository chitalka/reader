Chitalka 2.02.01

Two bugs fixed:

• EPUB page numbers could remain frozen in Safari while the book continued to turn. They now move with the content on macOS and iOS.
• Ordinary page turns and hiding or showing the reading controls could restart page-count calculation. Completed pagination is now reused and recalculated only when the book geometry changes.

Demo: https://chitalka.github.io/demo/
