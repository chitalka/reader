Chitalka 2.02.02

Large EPUB books now work more smoothly in Safari.

The reading footer appears immediately with the current pages and reading percentage, while the exact page count and completion forecast continue calculating in the background.

Background pagination is lighter and no longer measures completed chapters twice.
