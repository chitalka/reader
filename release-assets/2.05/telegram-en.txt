Chitalka 2.05

• Refined typography and page margins.
• Reading settings now include a live text preview, theme cards, and alignment choices.
• Collapsible contents highlight the current chapter.
• A new mobile bottom toolbar appears with a tap, without shifting the page.
• A larger tiger-face icon on a light background for the iPhone Home Screen.

Also fixed text disappearing when controls hide and improved keyboard navigation.
